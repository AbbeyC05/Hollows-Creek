Family Profile

The Beaumont Family

Family Overview

The Beaumont family are the wealthiest and most powerful family in Hollow's Creek. Lukas Beaumont is the Marquess and Mayor of Hollow's Creek and owns most buildings across the North and South sides of town. Each member of the family begins working from the age of fourteen and builds success of their own.

The family have appeared on their own television show since 1973, filmed and broadcast live twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. Lukas founded Beaumont Enterprises at fourteen; it has since grown into the world's largest company, operating across every area of business.

Family HeadLukas Henry Beaumont
ResidenceBeaumont Manor
LocationApprox. one hour outside Hollow's Creek
Major BusinessBeaumont Enterprises
TelevisionLive family series, 1973–present

Family Members

Major Family Business

Beaumont Enterprises

Founded by Lukas Beaumont at age fourteen, Beaumont Enterprises grew into the world's largest company, spanning every area of business and forming the centre of the Beaumont family's commercial influence.
